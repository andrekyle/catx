Returns Policy

Last updated: October 2025

At Brand Cartel, your satisfaction is our priority. We offer a hassle-free returns policy to ensure you're completely happy with your purchase. Please review our returns policy below for complete details.

Return Period

30-Day Return Policy: You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return most items for a full refund or exchange. The item must be in its original condition, unused, and in original packaging.

What Can Be Returned

Returnable Items

  • • Clothing and accessories (with tags attached)
  • • Electronics (in original packaging)
  • • Home and garden items
  • • Books and media
  • • Toys and games (unopened)
  • • Sports equipment (unused)

Non-Returnable Items

  • • Personal care items (opened)
  • • Underwear and swimwear
  • • Perishable goods
  • • Custom or personalized items
  • • Gift cards
  • • Digital downloads

Return Conditions

Item Condition

Items must be returned in their original condition, unused, and with all original packaging, tags, and accessories included.

Proof of Purchase

You must provide proof of purchase (order confirmation email or receipt) to process your return.

How to Return Items

1

Contact Customer Service

Email us at returns@brandcartel.co.za or call 0861 BRAND CARTEL (746748) to initiate your return request.

2

Receive Return Instructions

We'll send you a return authorization number and detailed instructions on how to return your item(s).

3

Package Your Item

Securely package the item(s) in original packaging with all accessories and include the return authorization number.

4

Ship Your Return

Use the prepaid return label provided or drop off at one of our collection points. We recommend using a trackable shipping method.

Return Shipping

Free Returns

Returns are free for defective items, wrong items sent, or if we made an error with your order.

Customer Returns

For change of mind returns, a R45 return shipping fee will be deducted from your refund.

Refund Process

Processing Time

Once we receive your returned item, we'll inspect it and process your refund within 3-5 business days.

Refund Method

Refunds will be processed using the same payment method used for the original purchase. Bank transfers may take 3-7 business days to reflect.

Partial Refunds

Items returned in used condition, without original packaging, or after the return period may receive a partial refund at our discretion.

Exchanges

We offer exchanges for different sizes or colors of the same item, subject to availability. Follow the same return process and indicate you'd like an exchange instead of a refund. If there's a price difference, we'll refund or charge the difference accordingly.

Defective or Damaged Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item, please contact us immediately at support@brandcartel.co.za with photos of the damage.

Currently, we only process returns within South Africa. International customers should contact our customer service team for special arrangements on returns.
