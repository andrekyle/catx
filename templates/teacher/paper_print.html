SUGGESTIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS TO MARKERS

  • Revisit the questions and the marking guidelines frequently during the marking session.
  • Read the question and the candidate's response together, so as not to be misled by phrasing.
  • A tick () marks each fact that earns a mark. Generally ONE mark per fact.
  • Accept correct answers expressed differently, e.g. 'slow' vs 'not fast'.
  • Mark the first N facts where N facts are required (e.g. first TWO facts if TWO are asked).
  • Where the marker symbol shows ✓✓ (Any two), award a mark per acceptable fact up to the limit.
