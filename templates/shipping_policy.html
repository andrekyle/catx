Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2025

Overview

At Brand Cartel, we strive to deliver your orders as quickly and efficiently as possible. We offer multiple shipping options to suit your needs, whether you need your items urgently or prefer a more economical delivery method.

Shipping Options

Standard Shipping

Delivery Time: 5-7 business days

Cost: R50 (Free on orders over R500)

Tracking: Available

Our most popular option, perfect for regular deliveries. Your items will be carefully packaged and shipped via our trusted courier partners.

Express Shipping

Delivery Time: 2-3 business days

Cost: R120

Tracking: Real-time tracking available

Need it faster? Choose express shipping for priority handling and quicker delivery. Ideal for gifts or urgent purchases.

Overnight Shipping

Delivery Time: Next business day

Cost: R200

Tracking: Premium tracking with SMS updates

Cutoff Time: Order by 2:00 PM for next-day delivery

Our fastest option for when you need it tomorrow. Orders placed before 2:00 PM on weekdays will be delivered the next business day.

Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or public holidays.

If we are experiencing a high volume of orders, shipments may be delayed by a few days. We will notify you if this occurs and provide updated delivery estimates.

Shipping Destinations

We currently ship to all addresses within South Africa, including:

  • Major cities and metropolitan areas
  • Suburban areas
  • Rural areas (may require additional delivery time)
  • PO Box addresses (Standard Shipping only)

Note: Overnight and Express shipping options may not be available for all remote locations.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can track your package using:

  • Your Brand Cartel account - My Orders section
  • The tracking link in your shipping confirmation email
  • Our courier partner's website using your tracking number

Lost or Damaged Shipments

While rare, sometimes packages can be lost or damaged during transit. If this happens:

  • Contact us immediately at support@brandcartel.co.za
  • Provide your order number and tracking information
  • We will investigate with our courier partner
  • You will receive a refund or replacement within 5-7 business days

All shipments are insured, and we will work quickly to resolve any issues.

Questions About Shipping?

If you have any questions about our shipping policy or need assistance with your order, please don't hesitate to contact us.
